**Three is a beautiful number.**
> Like The Godfather trilogy. Like the Dollars Trilogy — I just rewatched Il buono, il brutto, il cattivo (The Good, the Bad and the Ugly), Sergio Leone's 1966 masterpiece, the final chapter of his Man with No Name trilogy.
> The Japanese rōnin and samurai.
> The ancient Chinese wandering knight (youxia) and the jianghu of swift vengeance and honor.
> The American cowboy and bounty hunter of the Wild West.
>Three archetypes, separated by time and space — the first two born of the age of cold steel — yet converging at the same crossroads. Each carries an intoxicating shadow of romantic idealism, individual heroism, and anarchism. They ride for the bounty, yet the moment it is claimed, they dream only of retreat: to vanish into seclusion, to withdraw from the world's petty wars.
> And this brings me back to the very purpose of wealth: *using economic tools to isolate oneself from fools.*
