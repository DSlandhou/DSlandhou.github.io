The thrill of self-consciousness can be not only fleeting, but also enduring.
The Happy Person's Yield：The principle that the fortunate should retreat holds true at every stage. At its core, our genes render us unequal.
 A purpose of wealth is to avoid fools — to shun their company, their conversation, and the karma of others.
 In the first two decades, one truth is inescapable: every platform, however it filters, still harbors them.
Only after leaving these platforms （ once capital is secured ） can economic tools erect the necessary distance.
